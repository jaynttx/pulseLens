PulseLens is a technology demo, not a medical device. All processing happens in your browser.

About PulseLens

PulseLens uses remote photoplethysmography (rPPG). Every heartbeat pushes a little more blood into the skin of your face, which changes its colour by an amount far too small to see. A normal webcam can pick up that change, and from its rhythm we can work out your heart rate without touching you.

The signal is read by a lightweight state-space model (SSM), a neural network built to follow signals that change over time. It is only about 4 MB, takes a few milliseconds per frame and runs entirely in your browser. No video ever leaves your device.

Tips for an accurate reading

  • Use bright, even light on your face; avoid strong backlight and flickering lamps.
  • Sit still, look at the camera and avoid talking for 10–20 seconds.
  • Keep your whole face in the frame, about an arm's length from the camera.
  • Wait until Signal quality shows "Good" before trusting the number.
